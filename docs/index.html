Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 1-click duplication process work? ▼

When you click the template duplication link, Looker Studio prompts you to "Make a Copy" directly into your private Google workspace. You can preview all charts immediately using our pre-loaded synthetic demo data, then click "Select Data Source" to connect your own GA4 property or BigQuery view in 2 clicks.

Do I need BigQuery, or can I connect directly to GA4? ▼

You can use both. For small to mid-sized sites, the templates work out of the box with Looker Studio's native free GA4 connector. For high-traffic properties experiencing quota errors, we include the exact pre-computed BigQuery unnest SQL views to run with zero sampling and zero timeouts.

Yes. The Commercial ($129) and Agency ($289) licenses grant you full commercial rights to customize branding, logo, and colors, and deliver reports to unlimited clients with zero recurring fees.
